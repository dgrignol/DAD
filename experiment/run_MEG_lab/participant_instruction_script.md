# Participant Instruction Script (DAD Dot Task)

- Welcome participant
- In this task, you will see a single dot moving inside a square area while you keep your eyes on a central fixation cross.
- Your main goal is to keep fixation at the center and follow the task instructions carefully.
- Most trials require no response: just watch the moving dot while maintaining fixation.
- On some trials, a question appears after the motion: "Did the trajectory change its course?"
- When that question appears, you have 4s to respond. At the end of every block, you will see your responce accuracy for that block. Try to be as accurate as possible.
- Response mapping is: YES = Blue button (or key 8), NO = Red button (or key 1).
- If you are unsure, choose your best answer rather than waiting too long.
- Please try to minimize blinks and large eye movements during dot motion.
- You can blink during pauses, message screens, and blink-break screens.
- Short blink breaks may appear with a countdown ("Blink break", "Restart in:"); use them to rest your eyes.
- The experiment is divided into blocks, and brief messages appear between blocks.
- Try to stay still and comfortable, and keep your head position stable.
- If at any point you feel discomfort or need help, tell the experimenter immediately.
- We will now do a short start and then begin the first block.
